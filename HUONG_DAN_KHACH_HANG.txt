SEEDANCE AUTO - HUONG DAN CAI DAT
================================

1. Giai nen TOAN BO thu muc phat hanh vao mot thu muc rieng.
2. Khong doi ten, di chuyen hoac sua cac thu muc browser, extensions.
3. Chay SeedanceAuto.exe.
4. Nhap API key duoc cap. Gui Ma may hien trong Cai dat cho quan tri vien de kich hoat.
5. Chrome, FFmpeg va extension duoc tu dong nhan; khong can chon duong dan.

Neu ung dung bao tai nguyen bi thay doi/hong, hay xoa ban cu va giai nen lai
nguyen ven bo phat hanh. Moi tai khoan Dola su dung mot profile rieng tren may.

Khong chia se API key. Key duoc rang buoc voi ma may va may chu co the thu hoi.
